# PermitPilot

PermitPilot helps small contractors, remodelers, and homeowners organize local building-permit requirements into clear, step-by-step application checklists. Customers upload project details, receive jurisdiction-specific document guidance, deadline reminders, and optional human review—without PermitPilot acting as a legal or architectural authority. Its viral hook is a free shareable “permit complexity score” and downloadable checklist that users can post or send to contractors, while paid upgrades provide tailored research and ongoing tracking.

**Category:** SaaS
**Target Audience:** Independent contractors, home remodelers, property investors, and homeowners managing small construction projects in permit-heavy cities
**Revenue Model:** Freemium lead generation, $19-$49 per project checklist, $79-$149 human-reviewed application package, and $29-$99/month multi-project tracking subscriptions for contractors
**Viability Score:** 84/100
